Our story

Life with pets is the best. The fur on the couch? Less so.

Latrun started with a simple idea: the joy of having a pet shouldn't come with daily mess and stress. So we look for simple, well-made products that fix the small, everyday annoyances, and we skip the rest.

Our principles

What we believe in

01

One problem, one product

We only sell things that solve a real, everyday problem: fur, mud, boredom, messy walks. If it doesn't earn its place in your home, it's not in our shop.

02

Pet-safe and easy to clean

Silicone, fleece and sturdy plastics that are gentle on pets and easy to wash. No fragile parts and no batteries to replace.

03

Happy pet, or your money back

If your pet doesn't love it, tell us within 30 days and we'll make it right. See our returns policy.

Free advice, always

Not every pet problem needs a product. Our Journal is full of free routines, DIY games and honest advice, including when it's time to call the vet.
