# Latrun Pet Store — Website (v1 draft)
Built 2026-10-09. A static HTML/CSS site in English, made for previewing the brand and content before launch.

## Pages
- `index.html`: home (hero, problems → products, bundles, video slots, blog teasers, newsletter)
- `shop.html`: 12 products with explanations, how-to-use notes and the 3 bundles
- `blog.html` + `blog/`: 6 SEO articles
  - remove-pet-hair-from-couch (target: "how to get pet hair off couch")
  - reduce-dog-shedding ("how to reduce dog shedding")
  - clean-muddy-dog-paws ("how to clean muddy dog paws")
  - indoor-enrichment-for-dogs ("indoor enrichment for dogs")
  - dog-eating-too-fast ("dog eating too fast")
  - dog-walk-essentials-checklist ("what to bring on a dog walk")
- `about.html`, `faq.html` (shipping, returns, care)
- Video scripts: `../videos/video-scripts.md`

## Before launch: decisions and checks still open
1. **Platform:** Shopify (or similar) is needed for checkout. Copy the content in, or adapt this design into a theme. **Paid: needs NN's approval.**
2. **Domain and brand check:** confirm "Latrun" is available as a domain and on social handles, and isn't trademarked in the pet category in the target market.
3. **Target market:** the US, Canada or Israel. This drives suppliers, shipping times and currency.
4. **Placeholders in faq.html:** processing and delivery days, ship-to countries, support email.
5. **30-day guarantee and "Bundle & save" claims:** these are business decisions. Confirm before going live.
6. **Product specs:** verify every claim against the real supplier listing before publishing: BPA-free, dishwasher safe, folds under 2 cm, closure type, suction base, washable.
7. **Prices:** draft retail prices. Confirm margins once real supplier quotes are in.
8. **Images and videos:** emoji art and video slots are placeholders. Replace them with real product photos and clips (see the video scripts).
9. **Newsletter form:** a demo only. Connect it to Shopify Email or Klaviyo.
10. **No fake reviews:** add real reviews only after the first orders arrive.
