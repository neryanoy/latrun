🧼 Product care & safety

Are your products safe for my pet?

Our products are made from pet-safe materials like silicone and fleece. As with any toy or feeder, supervise your pet and remove items that become damaged.

How do I clean the lick mat and slow feeder?

Rinse with warm soapy water or place on the top rack of the dishwasher. For stuck-on food, soak for a few minutes first.

Can the snuffle mat go in the washing machine?

Yes. Shake out any food first, then wash on a gentle cycle and let it air dry.

Do your products replace advice from a vet?

No. Our products and blog tips are for everyday comfort and fun. For health concerns such as sudden shedding, eating changes or paw injuries, please see your veterinarian.
