What to Bring on a Dog Walk: The Essentials Checklist

🦮 Walks & Outdoors · 5 min read

Leash in one hand, phone in the other, keys in your mouth and you've forgotten the bags again. Sound familiar? A walk goes much more smoothly when everything has its place. Here's exactly what to bring, from a quick lap around the block to a full-day hike.

Every walk: the core four

  • Poop bags in a dispenser clipped to the leash, so they're always there. Pack two more than you think you'll need.
  • Treats in a pouch you can open with one hand. Rewarding calm walking, a good recall or ignoring another dog is how good habits form.
  • ID tags on the collar with a current phone number.
  • Your phone, for emergencies (and for the inevitable cute photos).

Walks longer than 30 minutes, or warm days

  • Water: dogs can overheat faster than people. A dog water bottle with a built-in trough lets them drink without you carrying a separate bowl.
  • A collapsible bowl for water or a snack at the park or a café. It folds flat and clips onto the leash.

Hikes and day trips

  • Plenty of water (roughly 1 liter per dog for a few hours in mild weather, more in heat)
  • A meal or high-value snacks
  • A towel for muddy paws before getting back in the car
  • A basic first-aid kit: gauze, a vet wrap bandage and tweezers for ticks and thorns
  • A long line, if your dog's recall isn't reliable yet

Signs your dog needs a break

Heavy panting, lagging behind, lying down on cool ground or looking for shade all mean it's time to rest and offer water. Short-nosed breeds (like Pugs and Bulldogs), seniors and puppies tire and overheat more quickly.

⚠️ Excessive drooling, wobbliness, vomiting or very red gums on a hot day can be signs of heatstroke, which is an emergency. Move your dog to shade, offer cool (not ice-cold) water and contact a vet immediately.

The 10-second pre-walk check

  1. Bags in the dispenser? ✅
  2. Treat pouch filled? ✅
  3. Water bottle full (for long walks)? ✅
  4. Collar snug and tag on? ✅

Keep everything clipped together on a hook by the door, and you'll never leave without it again.
